Does the blocking still help with several threads? 

Yes. The blocking provides a significant performance improvement across all tested thread configurations (1 to 8 threads).

The layer condition per thread explains the chaotic behavior at the right end of the graph:
For moderate row lengths, more threads lead to an earlier performance drop because the shared L3 cache is exhausted faster by concurrent threads.

However, for the longest rows (N_x = 2097152), the grid becomes extremely flat (N_y = 8). Since OpenMP splits these few rows among the threads, each thread only processes 1 or 2 rows. 
This eliminates vertical cache eviction for the unblocked version, causing the multi-threaded unblocked curves to slightly recover at the very edge.
